About Teck Tai

Teck Tai: Christmas decoration manufacturer for importers, wholesalers and retailers

Teck Tai designs and manufactures Christmas decorations for importers, wholesalers and retail buyers. The factory was founded in 1988.

Our sales office is in Hong Kong. Our own factory covers 35,000 m² in Qiaotou, Dongguan, China, where about 300 people work in design, development and production.

We make Christmas baubles, shaped ornaments, ornament sets and gift packs, garlands, wreaths and door swags, Christmas trees and tree toppers. Our three designers develop 30–40 new designs every season, and we also make ranges to customers’ briefs.

Our customers are importers, wholesalers and retail chains in Europe, North America and Southeast Asia, including the United Kingdom, Germany, the Netherlands, France, Italy, the United States and Canada. A UK home décor importer has worked with us for 15 years.

amfori BSCI is a social audit of working conditions in the factory, such as working hours, pay, and health and safety. It gives a rating from A to E; it is not a certificate.
