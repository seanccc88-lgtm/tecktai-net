About the Luxury style

Luxury is a look: velvet and flocked baubles, deep jewel tones, gold detail, sequins and thread-wrapped finishes. This page brings together the designs in our range with that look, across baubles, shaped ornaments, sets and gift packs. Designs can be developed to your brief and packed for your shelves. Questions about luxury Christmas decorations

What do you mean by luxury Christmas decorations?

We mean the look: velvet, jewel tones, gold detail and heavier finishes such as sequins and thread wrapping. It describes the style of the designs.

Can a luxury design be developed to our brief?

Yes. Colours and packaging can be developed to your brief for bulk orders. Tell us what you need and we send a quote within 24 hours, Monday to Friday (Hong Kong time).

Can you supply matching garlands, wreaths and toppers?

Yes. We make garlands, wreaths and door swags, Christmas trees and tree toppers as well as baubles, so you can range one look across several categories.
