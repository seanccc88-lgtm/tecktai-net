About the Modern style

Modern is a look: clean lines, geometric shapes and solid colour, from faceted and laser-cut baubles to striped baubles and finials, faceted snowflakes and mirror-ball sets. This page brings together the designs in our range with that look, across baubles, shaped ornaments, sets and bottle-brush trees. Designs can be developed to your brief, in your colours and packaging.

Questions about modern Christmas decorations

What do you mean by modern Christmas decorations?

We mean the look: clean lines, geometric and faceted shapes, and solid or metallic colours. It describes the style of the designs.

Can modern designs be made in our own colours?

Yes. Colours and packaging can be developed to your brief for bulk orders.

Can you supply matching trees, garlands and toppers?

Yes. We make Christmas trees, garlands, wreaths and tree toppers as well as baubles and ornaments, so you can range one look across several categories.
