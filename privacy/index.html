Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Tak Sam (Hong Kong) Company Limited, trading as Teck Tai, a Christmas decoration maker with an office in Hong Kong and a factory in China. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Our address is Flat R, 12/F, Valiant Industrial Centre, 2–12 Au Pui Wan Street, Fo Tan, Sha Tin, New Territories, Hong Kong. For any privacy question, email martin@tecktai.com.cn.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or sign up for the season preview: the details you type (company, name, email, country, message and any tick boxes or answers), the products on your quote list, the page you sent it from, how you say you found us, and the campaign tags in the link you came from, if there were any. If you accept analytics: which pages are visited and how, in a form that does not name you. We collect nothing you did not type or choose, and we do not ask for files.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, send quotes and samples, and handle any order that follows; to send the season preview if you asked for it; and, with your consent, to see which pages are useful. Answering your enquiry is the basis for using your details. Analytics rests on your consent, which you can withdraw. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run this website. Google: your enquiry goes through Google Apps Script into a Google Sheet, and the emails to you and to us go through Google's mail service. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics receives page visits. GitHub Pages hosts the website and sees the address and browser details of every visitor, as any web host does. Each uses the data only to provide that service to us. Your enquiry is read by our team in Hong Kong and China, so it is handled outside the UK and the EU.

Cookies and analytics

The cookie banner asks whether you accept Google Analytics. Until you accept, no analytics cookie is set and nothing is sent to Google Analytics. If you reject, the site works the same. We do not use advertising cookies. Your choice is remembered in your browser's storage on this site; clear it and the banner asks again.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and sign-ups are kept for 24 months, then deleted. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw your consent to analytics at any time. Email martin@tecktai.com.cn and we will act on it, normally within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to your local data protection authority.
