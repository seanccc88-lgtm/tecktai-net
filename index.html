Teck Tai at a glance

Christmas decoration manufacturer since 1988.

Teck Tai designs and manufactures Christmas decorations for importers, wholesalers and retailers. Founded in 1988, we are a Hong Kong company with our own 35,000 m² factory in Qiaotou, Dongguan, China, and about 300 staff in design, development and production. We make Christmas baubles, shaped ornaments, ornament sets, garlands, wreaths, Christmas trees and tree toppers for customers in the UK, Germany, the Netherlands, France, Italy, the US and Canada. Ranges are developed to your brief and packed under your brand. The factory is amfori BSCI audited, and your own QC is welcome before shipment. Quotes within 24 hours, Monday to Friday (Hong Kong time).
