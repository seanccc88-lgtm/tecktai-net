What does an amfori BSCI audit cover, and what can a buyer ask for?

The Teck Tai team · Published 5 October 2026 · Updated 20 November 2026

Short answer

An amfori BSCI audit checks working conditions in a factory, such as working hours, pay, health and safety, and the absence of child and forced labour. The factory receives a rating from A to E, not a certificate. A buyer can ask the factory for the audit date, the rating, the audit company and the report.

What the audit is

amfori BSCI is a social audit programme run by amfori, a business association of retailers, importers and brands. Independent audit companies visit the factory, interview workers and check records against the amfori Code of Conduct.

Are amfori and BSCI the same?

Not quite. amfori is the business association; BSCI is its social audit programme, so the full name is amfori BSCI. Many buyers simply say “BSCI”.

What it covers

The audit looks at how people are treated at work: working hours and overtime, fair pay, occupational health and safety, no child labour, no forced labour, no discrimination, freedom of association, and how the factory manages all of this day to day. It does not test the products themselves; product safety is covered by separate test reports.

How the rating works

RatingMeaning
AVery good
BGood
CAcceptable
DInsufficient: a follow-up audit is required
EUnacceptable: a follow-up audit is required

The programme does not issue certificates, so the correct wording is “amfori BSCI audited”. A supplier calling itself “BSCI certified” is using the wrong term.

What to ask your supplier

  1. The date of the latest audit.
  2. The overall rating, A to E.
  3. The name of the audit company.
  4. A copy of the audit report, or access to it on the amfori platform if you are a member.
