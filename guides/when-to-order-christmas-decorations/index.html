When to order Christmas decorations for Christmas 2027

The Teck Tai team · Published 20 November 2026 · Updated 20 November 2026

Short answer

For Christmas 2027, plan to choose your range around the January trade fairs, and confirm orders and approve samples before or soon after Chinese New Year (China's public holiday runs from 5 to 12 February 2027). Ask your factory for its production calendar and its last date for confirming orders before you finalise quantities.

The 2027 calendar at a glance

DateWhat happens
10–13 JanuaryHarrogate Christmas & Gift, UK (trade only)
29 January – 2 FebruaryChristmasworld, Frankfurt
5–12 FebruaryChinese New Year public holiday in China (New Year’s Day: 6 February)
7–10 FebruarySpring Fair, NEC Birmingham
15 April – 5 MayCanton Fair, spring session, Guangzhou (phase dates published by the organiser)
1–7 OctoberNational Day holiday in China

Before the January fairs

Decide which categories you want to develop for 2027, gather your colour ideas and reference photos, and shortlist the factories you want to meet or ask for samples. A clear brief makes the fair meetings far more useful.

Around Chinese New Year

Factories in China close for the public holiday, and many close for longer than the official dates while staff travel home and back. Confirm designs and quantities before the holiday where you can, and ask your factory for its exact closing and reopening dates.

Spring: samples, packaging and production plans

Approve samples and packaging artwork, and agree the production schedule and shipping date with your factory. Changes are easiest to make at this stage; late changes can move your shipping date.

Summer and autumn: production and shipment

Production, quality checks, packing and shipment follow the schedule you agreed. Plan freight with your forwarder early, and remember China’s National Day holiday in the first week of October.
