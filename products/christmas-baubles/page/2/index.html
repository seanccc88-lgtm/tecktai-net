About Christmas baubles

Plastic Christmas baubles and finials from Teck Tai, for importers, wholesalers and retail buyers. The range covers round, faceted and egg-shaped baubles, drops and finials, from shiny, matte and glitter to mirror, velvet, sequin, pearl and snow-frosted. Most designs can be supplied loose, with hanging caps, or boxed for retail, and colours and styles can be developed to your brief for bulk orders.

Finishes and styles

Classic shiny, matte and glitter baubles sit alongside mirror balls, velvet flocking, sequins, silk wrapping, laser-cut patterns and snow-frosted finishes. Each design is tagged by finish and theme, so a range can be built around one look, such as Sparkle & Mirror or Velvet & Jewel.

Colours

Standard colours are listed on each product page; colours and styles can be developed to your brief for bulk orders.

Packing and packaging

Baubles can be packed loose with hanging caps, in clear PET boxes and tubes, in kraft window boxes or in shelf-ready counter displays. Pieces per pack and carton details are listed per product as they are confirmed.

Ordering and documents

Production follows sample approval. The factory is amfori BSCI audited, and test reports for plastic baubles are available on request. For a quote, tell us the item numbers, quantities and colours you need.

Questions about Christmas baubles

How are Christmas baubles packed?

Loose with hanging caps, in clear PET boxes and tubes, in kraft window boxes or in counter displays, depending on the design.

Can the baubles be made to our brief?

Yes. Colours and styles can be developed to your brief, for bulk orders.
