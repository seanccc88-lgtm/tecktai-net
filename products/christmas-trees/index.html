About Christmas trees

Our Christmas trees range from mini trees for desks, shelves and store counters to full-size artificial trees. The tabletop trees come plain, flocked or fully decorated, in several heights per design; full-size trees are made to order, so ask us for heights and specifications.

Tree styles

Natural green and snow-flocked trees on wood-slice or burlap bases, bottle-brush trees in green and metallic tones, cone trees built from baubles or tinsel, decorated tabletop trees dressed with baubles, pinecones and bows, and full-size artificial trees to order.

Colours and decorations

Decoration colours and combinations can be developed to your brief for bulk orders.

Packing

Trees are boxed individually or as graduated sets. Heights and carton details are listed per product as they are confirmed.

Ordering and documents

Production follows sample approval. The factory is amfori BSCI audited, and documents are available on request.

Questions about Christmas trees

Which heights are available?

Most designs come in several heights. Tell us the heights you need and we will confirm them with the quote.

Can the decorations be made to our brief?

Yes. Decoration colours and combinations can be developed to your brief, for bulk orders.

Ready to build your seasonal range with us?

Tell us which items you're interested in and we'll send a quote, with samples if you need them.
