About Christmas wreaths & door swags

Christmas wreaths and door swags from Teck Tai, made in our own factory for importers, wholesalers and retail chains. We supply decorated artificial wreaths at 30, 45 and 60 cm, door swags and teardrop swags for narrow doors and pillars, and wreaths and topiaries built entirely from baubles. Each is decorated with pine, baubles, pinecones, berries and bows and arrives ready to hang. Colour schemes, from red and gold to cream, pastel and themed looks, are developed to your brief for wholesale and bulk orders, and can match our baubles and garlands.

Sizes and shapes

Round wreaths at 30, 45 and 60 cm, door swags and teardrop swags for narrow doors and pillars, and wreaths, topiaries and garlands built entirely from baubles.

Colour schemes

Red, gold, cream, bronze, pastel and themed schemes such as gingerbread or candy cane. Colours, sizes and arrangements can be specified for each order.

Packing

Wreaths and swags are supplied ready to hang. Carton details are listed per product as they are confirmed.

Ordering and documents

Production follows sample approval. The factory is amfori BSCI audited, and documents are available on request.

Questions about Christmas wreaths

Which wreath sizes are available?

Decorated wreaths come at 30, 45 and 60 cm, plus door swags and teardrop swags.

Can the colour scheme be made to our brief?

Yes. Colours, sizes and arrangements can be specified for each order.

Can wreaths be matched to our baubles and garlands?

Yes. We make the baubles and garlands too, so a wreath can carry the same colours and decorations as the rest of your range.
