About Christmas ornament sets & gift packs

Ornament sets and gift packs are where the packaging sells the product. Our sets combine baubles, finials and shaped pieces in one colour scheme, packed in clear, kraft or shaped boxes that go straight onto the shelf.

Set formats

Clear tubes and boxes, zip bags with handles, kraft window and handle boxes, gift boxes, shaped star, tree and house boxes, and pre-packed counter displays.

Contents and colours

Sets mix sizes, shapes and finishes in one colour story. Colours, contents and box formats can be developed to your brief for bulk orders.

Kraft and paper packaging

Kraft boxes and sleeves keep the baubles visible through a window while reducing the plastic on shelf, a format that suits Eco Kraft and natural ranges.

Ordering and documents

Production follows sample approval. The factory is amfori BSCI audited, and documents are available on request. For a quote, tell us the item numbers, quantities and packaging you need.

Questions about Christmas ornament sets

Can the set contents and box be made to our brief?

Yes. Colours, contents and box formats can be developed to your brief, for bulk orders.

Which box formats are available?

Clear tubes and boxes, kraft window and handle boxes, shaped star, tree and house boxes, and counter displays.
