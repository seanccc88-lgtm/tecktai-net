About the range

Teck Tai makes Christmas decorations only, for importers, wholesalers and retail buyers. The range covers plastic Christmas baubles and finials, shaped Christmas ornaments, ornament sets with retail packaging, garlands, wreaths and door swags, mini Christmas trees and tree toppers. Colours and styles can be developed to your brief, and supplied in clear, kraft or shaped boxes and counter displays; which items can be customised is confirmed per product.

Buyer questions

Can you make products to our brief?

Yes. Colours and styles can be developed to your brief, for bulk orders.

Which packaging options are there?

Clear boxes, tubes, header bags, kraft window boxes, shaped boxes and counter displays.

Which documents can you supply?

The amfori BSCI audit report and test reports are available on request.

How do I place an order?

Send us the item numbers, quantities and colours through the quote form, by email or on WhatsApp. We send a quote within 24 hours, Monday to Friday (Hong Kong time).

Can you make something not shown on the site?

Yes. The site shows a selection of our range. Send us a photo or a sample and we'll quote.

Ready to build your seasonal range with us?

Tell us which items you're interested in and we'll send a quote, with samples if you need them.
