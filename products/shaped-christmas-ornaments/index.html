About shaped Christmas ornaments

Shaped ornaments give a Christmas range its story. Our shapes cover the classic figures buyers ask for, from Santa Claus and reindeer to candy canes, gingerbread and stars, in glitter, painted, shiny and velvet finishes.

Shapes and themes

Each design is tagged by finish and theme, so a range can be built around one story, such as Sweet Treats, Woodland, Vintage or Winter White. Clip-on birds, candles and butterflies fix to branches, wreaths and garlands without hooks.

Colours

Standard colours are listed on each product page; colours and styles can be developed to your brief for bulk orders.

Packing and packaging

Shaped ornaments can be supplied singly with hangers, in header bags, in clear boxes and tubes or in boxed sets. Pieces per pack and carton details are listed per product as they are confirmed.

Ordering and documents

Production follows sample approval. The factory is amfori BSCI audited, and documents are available on request. For a quote, tell us the item numbers, quantities and colours you need.

Questions about shaped Christmas ornaments

How are shaped Christmas ornaments packed?

Singly with hangers, in header bags, in clear boxes and tubes or in boxed sets, depending on the design.

Can the ornaments be made to our brief?

Yes. Colours and styles can be developed to your brief, for bulk orders.
